Privacy Policy – Primbon Jawa

Last Updated: February 2025

Primbon Jawa is developed and published by Onbozo Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information through our mobile application.

Note: By using Primbon Jawa, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Primbon Jawa does not directly collect any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers. However, depending on the features you use:

  • Calendar Integration: With your explicit permission, the app accesses your device's calendar (READ_CALENDAR and WRITE_CALENDAR) solely to add and manage local event reminders (such as Selapan and Javanese birthdays) requested by you. User-inputted names and birth dates for reminders are stored locally on your device or in your device calendar and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

To keep the application free to use, we display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob uses third-party SDKs that may collect information to serve ads that are relevant to you.

The information collected by third-party services may include:

  • IP address (used to estimate general location).
  • Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA).
  • Device information (model, OS version, hardware settings).
  • App interaction data and crash logs.

3. Consent Management (GDPR & CCPA)

We comply with privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

  • UMP SDK: We use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent from users in the EEA, UK, and California.
  • User Choice: You can choose to allow or decline personalized advertising. If you decline, ads will still be shown but will not be based on your personal interests.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our records.

5. Data Security and Retention

We do not store any of your personal data or calendar information on our own servers. Data collected by AdMob is processed and secured by Google according to their infrastructure standards. Local reminder data stored via app preferences remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

6. Disclaimer

Primbon Jawa provides information based on traditional Javanese literature and cultural heritage. The content is intended for educational and cultural preservation purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial advice.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
